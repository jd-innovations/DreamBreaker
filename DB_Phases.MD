The Core Principle

The philosophy that has guided the project from the beginning is:

Build one subsystem completely.
Verify and audit it.
Freeze it (bug fixes only).
Move to the next subsystem.
Only after the platform is functionally complete, conduct a Design Sprint to polish the entire experience.

That disciplined approach is a big reason the project has remained organized despite its growing scope.
